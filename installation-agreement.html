3. What we supply and install (scope of works)

Subject to a site assessment, First Class Filtration agrees to supply and professionally install one (1) First Class Filtration whole-home inline water filtration system at the installation address specified on this Agreement. A standard installation includes:

  • A 2mm 316 marine-grade stainless steel backing plate and external cover, powder coated to your selected colour;
  • A copper and brass valve train and bypass assembly, brazed with silver solder;
  • A two-stage filter assembly — a stepped 25 to 5 micron sediment filter and a 1 micron carbon chloraguard filter;
  • Connection to your main water line by a licensed plumber; and
  • The initial set of filter cartridges.

The installation address, your selected cover colour, and the agreed installation location on your property are recorded on this Agreement and form part of it.
