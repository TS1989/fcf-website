1. Overview

First Class Filtration Pty Ltd ("we", "us") stands behind the quality of our systems and our installation work. This Warranty Policy sets out the coverage that applies to products and services provided by us.

This warranty is provided in addition to, and does not limit, any rights you may have under the Australian Consumer Law.

2. External cover — lifetime warranty

The external stainless steel powdercoated enclosure covering your filtration system is covered by a lifetime warranty against:

  • Structural failure of the cover or backing plate
  • Rust or corrosion arising from the 316 stainless steel construction or fixings
  • Failure of the powdercoat finish under normal Australian outdoor conditions (UV, rain, heat)

This warranty applies to the original installation address and is not transferable unless agreed to in writing by First Class Filtration.

The cover is constructed from 2mm 316 stainless steel, with all bolts, washers, anchors, and screws also in 316 stainless. The powder coat is applied via a 3-part process: sandblasted for adhesion profile, primed, then powdercoated in the selected colour.

3. Service-linked warranty renewal

First Class Filtration operates on a simple principle: the more you maintain your system with us, the more we stand behind it.

Filtration component coverage and installation workmanship warranty are renewed for a further 12 months each time your filter cartridges are replaced by a First Class Filtration technician. This means:

  • A customer who has their filters replaced annually by FCF will have their component warranty perpetually active — it never expires as long as the service relationship continues
  • Coverage is tied to FCF servicing. If filter changes are carried out by a third party or DIY, the 12-month renewal does not apply, though existing coverage does not immediately void
  • The renewal applies from the date of each completed FCF service, not the original installation date

This is our commitment to long-term customers. We build systems to last, and we want to keep them running.

6. Filter cartridges

Filter cartridges are consumable items and are not covered under this warranty. Their service life depends on water quality and household water usage. The FCF app provides usage-based reminders to help you maintain your system. Replacement cartridges are available through the FCF app or by contacting us directly.

7. What is not covered

This warranty does not cover:

  • Damage caused by misuse, neglect, or unauthorised modification of the system
  • Damage caused by events outside our control, including but not limited to flooding, fire, storm, acts of God, or third-party interference
  • Consumable parts, including filter cartridges
  • Work performed by parties other than First Class Filtration technicians
  • Normal wear and tear on any component
  • Aesthetic damage (e.g. scratches or minor marks) that does not affect the function of the product
  • Issues caused by failure to have filter cartridges replaced at recommended intervals
