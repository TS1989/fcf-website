Why it matters

Why whole-home filtration?

More than just the kitchen tap

A jug filter or under-sink unit only treats water at one point. Our inline system is installed directly on your main water line — so every drop of water entering your home is filtered before it reaches anything: taps, showers, washing machines, dishwashers, and hot water systems. That means you can remove every Brita jug, tap filter, and bench-top unit in your home — one system replaces them all, and then goes further.

What's in Adelaide's water?

Adelaide's mains water is treated with chlorine and chloramines at some of the highest concentrations in Australia, along with sediment, varying hardness, and emerging contaminants like PFAS. The FCF system's 1 micron carbon chloraguard stage is specifically chosen for its effectiveness against these chemicals — not just sediment. These things aren't always visible, but they affect the water your family uses every day.

Beyond drinking

  • Showers:  gentler on skin and hair — less chlorine exposure
  • Laundry:  reduced chemical exposure means less wear on fabrics
  • Appliances:  less scale buildup in kettles, dishwashers, and hot water systems
  • Cooking:  better-tasting water for everything you cook and drink

Pure water, every day

The difference clean water makes.

Most people think about water quality when they drink it. But your family uses water all day long — and that's where whole-home filtration really earns its place.

Children & family

Young children drink more water per kilogram of body weight than adults, and their developing systems are more susceptible to what's in it. Whole-home filtration means every glass they drink, every meal you prepare, and every bath they take starts with cleaner water.

Cooking & drinking

Water is in almost everything you cook — pasta, rice, soups, sauces, coffee, and tea. The flavour of your tap water directly affects the flavour of your food. Filtered water means better-tasting meals and drinks across the board, without buying bottled.

Washing fruit & vegetables

Chlorinated tap water doesn't strip pesticide residue as effectively as filtered water — and chlorine can chemically interact with some residues on produce. Washing your food in filtered water is a simple way to give your family a cleaner plate.

Your pets

Dogs and cats can be sensitive to chlorine and chloramines — the same chemicals you can smell in tap water. Many pet owners find their animals drink more willingly from filtered water. Better hydration supports kidney health and overall wellbeing, particularly in older pets.

Skin & hair

Chlorine strips the natural oils from your skin and scalp with every shower. If your skin feels dry or tight after washing, or your hair lacks moisture and shine, your mains water may be a contributing factor. Filtered water is noticeably softer — especially noticeable for sensitive skin and colour-treated hair.

Plants & garden

Chlorine is an antimicrobial agent — that's its job in the mains supply. But it doesn't stop working when you water your garden. Filtered water is gentler on soil bacteria, supports healthier root systems, and can make a noticeable difference for vegetable gardens and sensitive indoor plants.
